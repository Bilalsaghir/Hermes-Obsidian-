# Low Budget AI — Channel Brief

Not launched. Nothing published yet. Everything here is groundwork.

## Audience
Founders, SME owners, and developers. Technical enough to follow a terminal, unwilling to spend enterprise money to find out whether something works.

## Positioning
Real AI work on budgets that sound impossible — with the receipts. Not "top 10 AI tools". Actual builds, actual costs, actual failures.

The proof asset already exists: **an eight-agent team running on $18/month, on the same VPS that hosts a live client business.** Every mistake made building it is an episode.

## Format rules
- Every video names a real number: cost, time saved, tokens used.
- Show the failure before the fix. The debugging is the content.
- No affiliate-bait tool roundups. The channel's credibility is the asset.
- UK English. Plain language. Assume competence, not knowledge.

## First ten ideas, drawn from work already done
1. I ran 8 AI agents for a month on $18. Here's the full bill.

6. I gave an AI agent root on my server. Here's what I locked down first.
7. Finding 13GB of waste on a VPS in twenty minutes
8. Prompt injection is the real risk — and why my research agent is quarantined
9. Free AI models aren't free — reading the data policy that trades your prompts
10. One orchestrator, six specialists: why more agents doesn't mean more profiles

## What I research
- What founders and developers actually search for around cheap AI tooling
- Topics with real demand where existing videos are thin or outdated
- Competitor channels: what they publish, what performs, what they avoid
- **Gaps, not trends.** A crowded topic done better is worth less than an underserved one done first.
