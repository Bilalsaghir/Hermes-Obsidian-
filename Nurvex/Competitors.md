## Four findings that matter more than the individual profiles
1. **Three of them run "best UK AI agency" ranking blogs** — Antek, Ampliflow, Plexo Logic. A coordinated land-grab on buyer-intent search. Anyone googling "best AI automation agency UK" lands on a competitor's page that ranks the competitor. The single most actionable competitive fact.
2. **Nurvex is the odd one out on pricing.** Softomate (£6,500+), Flowio (£750+), Northloop (£950/£4,500/£1,800pm), Elevate (£3,000+) and AImpress (£1,500+) all publish. We don't. To an SME comparing five sites, opacity reads as expensive.
3. **Elevate publishes an ROI guarantee; Automee sells by subscription.** Both lower the buyer's perceived risk at the point of decision. Our Pilot tier competes against a monthly subscription with no commitment.
4. **Nobody else is running zero-data-retention routing across their own stack.** Softomate claims GDPR-first; we can demonstrate it. A stronger, more specific claim than theirs and already true.
